As mapped draws roads as they are. Knox County straight lays every road in straight runs along the tiles and on 45-degree diagonals. Vanilla curves routes them the way the game’s own B42 map draws its streets: along the tiles, 45 degrees and the 2:1 slopes, with its curves and street shapes. Built-up streets square keeps streets lined with buildings on the grid, as the buildings are, and off them.
